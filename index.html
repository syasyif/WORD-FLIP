HOW TO PLAY 🕹️

Welcome to WORD FLIP!

Your mission:

  • Read the scrambled words carefully.
  • Tap the words to move them into the answer area and build a correct English sentence.
  • Tap a word in the answer area to remove it.
  • Check your sentence before submitting.
  • Tap SUBMIT ANSWER to see if you are correct.

Rules:
